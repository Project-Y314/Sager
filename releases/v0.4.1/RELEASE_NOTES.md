# Sager v0.4.1 — Outline Tray Icon

The tray icon is now a clean **outline logo**: the Sager padlock drawn as thin white strokes with a cyan keyhole, on a transparent background — no more filled badge. It sits quietly and matches the black & white product design.

Also:
- The old v0.3-era installer leftovers are a thing of the past: Sager never registers itself anywhere without your say-so ("Start with Windows" lives in the dashboard, opt-in).
- Same single-exe, same one-page dashboard, same automatic save backups and Steam-style achievement toasts as v0.4.0.

Download `sager.exe`, double-click, and it appears in your tray.
